## What's new in v0.1.1

### Dark mode
White on black for every screen. Photos and cover art keep their normal
tones. Turn it on under **Screens → Dark mode**.

### Screens
- **Clock: year progress.** "Day 280 of 365 · Week 41" with a progress bar
  under the date (toggle under *Clock & weather*). (#4)
- **Game: session time.** How long you've been playing appears on the Game
  screen and the dashboard while a game runs, updating every minute. A new
  **Session timer** layout shows it as a large clock (*Screens → Game screen
  layout*). (#2)
- **Game: controller indicator.** A gamepad icon appears next to the title
  while a controller is connected, within seconds of turning it on or off.
  Works with Steam Controllers on the wireless puck and with USB and
  Bluetooth pads. (#3)
- **Performance: RAM history.** Memory joins CPU and GPU on the 30-minute
  graph, with a legend that matches what's drawn. (#5)
- **Performance: history survives restarts.** The graph no longer starts
  empty after a plugin update, Decky restart or reboot, and gaps (such as
  sleep) show as breaks instead of joined lines. (#6)
- **Calendar: week starts on your region's day.** Automatic uses your
  system's region setting (Sunday in the US, Monday in most of Europe,
  Saturday in much of the Middle East), from the official Unicode CLDR
  data; you can also pick Monday, Sunday or Saturday. Nothing is looked up
  online. (#8)
- **Photo frame: fill the screen.** Crop photos to fill the whole display
  instead of showing them whole with bars. (#10)

### Privacy and transparency
- New **[privacy page](https://github.com/v1k0d3n/monoink/blob/main/docs/privacy.md)**:
  everything monoink reads, stores and contacts, what it never does, and
  commands to verify it yourself.
- The service log is now readable only by your user account (it contains
  your display's Bluetooth address).
- Every network request now identifies itself as monoink.

### Smaller improvements and fixes
- On an idle machine, the CPU and GPU lines on the Performance graph no
  longer disappear into the graph's border.
- A new game's session reads "Just started" instead of "0m".
- The dashboard's game column no longer overflows into the system stats.
- New **[screen gallery](https://github.com/v1k0d3n/monoink/blob/main/docs/screens.md)**
  showing every screen in light and dark mode.

### Upgrading
Install the new version the same way as before. Your settings are kept. A
"Week starts on Sunday" choice is kept; otherwise the calendar switches to
Automatic.
